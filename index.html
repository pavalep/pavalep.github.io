Open media

Simba

An open-source media player for Android, in React Native and TypeScript. One typed adapter per catalogue — Podcast Index, Jamendo, Audius, LibriVox, Internet Archive, TVmaze, Radio Browser, MusicBrainz, IPTV — normalised into a single track and episode model.

  • libmpv playback engine, published as @simba-dev/react-native-media-player
  • MediaSession lock-screen controls, Picture-in-Picture, background audio
  • AI curation on the Gemini free tier, with a prompt registry and a Cloud Run service

Habit & somatic wellbeing

Habitrak

An offline-first wellbeing coach in Flutter, on Clean Architecture and BLoC. Everything it knows stays on the device, in an encrypted local vault. No account, no cloud, no paywall.

  • AES-256-CBC vault with SHA-256 key derivation
  • Box, 4-7-8 and coherent breathwork, generative sound baths, a mindful-walking tracker
  • A vector renderer that holds 120 FPS without triggering a garbage collection
